GALLE: India firmly seized control of the opening Test match against Sri Lanka, posting a formidable first-innings total of 460/9 before stumps on Day 2 at the Galle International Stadium.

The Indian batting lineup displayed supreme authority on a turning Galle surface, putting constant pressure on the hosts' bowling attack right from the opening session.

🏏 Key Match Highlights

Top order set the platform with a 150+ opening stand, followed by crucial middle-order contributions to push past the 450-run mark.

Top-Order Dominance Sets the Stage

Capitalizing on excellent batting conditions on Day 1 and early Day 2, the Indian top order executed clear plans against Sri Lanka’s spin arsenal. Crisp footwork and aggressive strike rotation prevented the Lankan spinners from settling into tight bowling rhythms.

Spinners Face Uphill Battle

Despite relentless efforts and late wickets towards the second session, the Sri Lankan bowling unit conceded key boundaries in the third session. With 460 runs on the board, the pitch is already showing signs of variable bounce, placing massive pressure on Sri Lanka's top order for Day 3.
