BCCI Confirms 15-Member Squad for Upcoming 3-Match T20I Series Against Afghanistan

📍 BCCI HQ, Mumbai • ⏱️ 2 min read • Today
Afghanistan Tour of India 3-Match T20I Series
India vs Afghanistan
📍 Arun Jaitley Stadium, Delhi (Sep 13, Sep 15, Sep 17)

MUMBAI: The Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) Senior Men's Selection Committee has officially announced a balanced 15-member squad for the upcoming three-match T20 International series against Afghanistan.

All three matches of the high-octane bilateral series are scheduled to take place at the Arun Jaitley Stadium in Delhi under lights, starting Sunday, September 13.

⚡ Key Squad Takeaways

The squad features an explosive mix of experienced core players alongside standout young performers, prioritizing aggressive batting and multi-dimensional spin-bowling all-rounders.

Official 15-Member India Squad

Batters & Wicketkeepers
Top-order heavy dynamic hitters, specialized powerplay stroke-makers, and explosive middle-order finishers.
All-Rounders
Hard-hitting pace bowling all-rounders and crafty left-arm spin options designed to control the middle overs.
Bowlers (Pace & Spin)
High-speed frontline seamers with proven death-over yorker execution, backed by wicket-taking wrist spinners.

Series Context & Preparation

With conditions in Delhi expected to favor fast-paced scoring and quality spin play, team management is eager to test tactical variations against a battle-tested Afghanistan lineup renowned for their world-class T20 talent.
